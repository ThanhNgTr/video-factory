# Effects in Video Factory

Every effect is data on a `Shot` (`packages/contracts/src/scene.ts`), drawn as a pure function of the frame. Nothing is fetched, nothing is generated by an AI model.

## What exists

| Effect | Data | Code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | `lighting`: normal, dim, flicker, blackout, red (silhouettes with eyes and teeth), headlights (a sweep across the set's windows) | `World.tsx` |
| Weather, flood | `fx`: rain, snow, water | `Weather.tsx`; `video.timeline` lays rain and wind beds under them |
| Story props | `props[].motion`: still, drop, sink, tilt, shake, float, grow, vanish, starting `at` s | `modules/video/src/domain/prop-motion.ts`; a drop thuds by itself |
| Camera hits | `camera` shake, zoom-shake; `move` handheld | `World.tsx` |
| Transitions | `transition` fade (through black), flash (white) | `Overlay.tsx` |
| Drips | `CastMember.drip` up (wrong) or down | `Character2D.tsx` |
| Sound | `sfx`: at most 3 cues a shot, `{id, at}`; ids in `library/sfx` (ding, door, flicker, footsteps, heartbeat, pop, rain, ring, stinger, thud, tick, ticks, whoosh, wind) | `video.timeline`, `audio.mix` |
| Graphics | count, scale, chart, formula (result circled by `Scribble`), list, icons, layers; springs for pops | `Graphics.tsx`, `domain/graphic-timing.ts` |
| Diorama | layers drop in with a thud, weather, a vehicle | `Diorama.tsx`, `dioramaDrops` |
| Captions | stressed words highlighted, unspoken words grey, style by format kind | `Overlay.tsx` |

## Using effects well

1. **Motivated.** The story causes every effect: the line says it rains, the monster is here, the box falls. An unasked-for effect is noise and teaches the viewer to ignore effects.
2. **The action reads without it.** Pose, prop motion and cut must work with the effect turned off; an effect accents a strong moment and never rescues a weak one. Its strength follows the force behind it: a heavy drop shakes more than a light one, and a beat of anticipation before the hit sells it.
3. **Show it, do not react to it.** When the narration says something appears, falls, sinks or breaks, a prop does it on screen (ADR 0022); a character frowning at something off screen is not the picture.
4. **Picture and sound on the same frame.** A prop's `at` and its sfx cue's `at` are the same second. A drop already thuds: do not add a second thud.
5. **Contrast makes the hit.** Quiet before loud, still before shake, normal light before red. A scare: build (dim or flicker, heartbeat, a tele lens or a slow push) → hit (stinger with zoom-shake, or red with a flash) → release (the light comes back dim). Red stays at most 3 shots in a row and a quarter of the video (`checkEditing`).
6. **Budget.** A repeated effect stops working: one flash per scare, shake only on impacts, at most 3 cues a shot.
7. **Comedy hits:** a pop on a prop that grows in, a whoosh on a fast exit, a ding on a eureka, then a still beat of 1-2 s after the punch line before the next cut.
8. **Readable.** Effects never cover a face or the caption band; rain goes with dim light.

## Adding an effect

1. **Contract:** an additive optional field or enum value in `scene.ts` (old scenes still parse, `schemaVersion` stays 1).
2. **Logic:** a pure function in `modules/video/src/domain/` (like `prop-motion.ts`) with a Vitest test. Deterministic: `random(seed)` from stable ids, no `Date.now`, `Math.random` or state across frames (`vf-motion`).
3. **Drawing:** in `World.tsx`, `Weather.tsx`, `Overlay.tsx` or `Graphics.tsx`. Renders run on CPU WebGL (swangle): keep shaders cheap and low-resolution (`shader-glsl`); no Remotion `effects` / `HtmlInCanvas`, no remote images.
4. **Sound:** if it makes one, cue it in `video.timeline` from the same domain function as the picture, the way `dioramaDrops` feeds both.
5. **Director:** one line in `direct.md` saying when to use it, with the version bumped; a rule in `checkEditing` if overuse is likely.
6. **Versions:** bump `video.render` (and `video.timeline` if the sound changes). Render stills at the effect's peak frame and look at them.
7. **New SFX:** a licensed audio file plus `{id, file, seconds, license}` JSON in `library/sfx` (`tools/make-audio-library.sh` makes the starter set).

Not built yet, each a cheap 2D addition through the steps above if a sample needs it: speed lines on a run, impact stars, sweat drops and anger marks by a face, a freeze frame with a title.
